Responsible use of AI

I work every day with LLMs and coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Kimi and others). They help me move faster, and my years of experience help me notice when they get things wrong. To use them well I follow a few rules:

  • I review and understand every change before accepting it. If I cannot explain it, it does not ship.
  • I do not share my clients' code, data or credentials with outside services without their permission.
  • Nothing reaches production without tests. I am responsible for what I ship, not the tool.
